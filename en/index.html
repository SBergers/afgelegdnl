HOW IT WORKS

From GPS track to a landscape on your desk.

01

Choose your ride

Pick a route from the collection, or upload your own GPX and give it a name: your first marathon, that one climb or simply your regular loop.

02

We design it together

We match your ride to real elevation data and make a first design. You take a look, we fine-tune it until it really is your map. We only print once you are happy.

03

Printed layer by layer

Your map is built up from hundreds of thin layers, with your route in a bold colour. Ready to give as a gift, or to admire yourself.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know.

When will my map be shipped?

Every map is made to order. A route from the collection ships within 3 to 5 working days of your order. For a map of your own ride we first make a preview, which you get within 1 to 3 working days. Once you approve it, your map ships within 3 to 5 working days. Delivery by the carrier comes on top of that; you get a tracking code so you can follow your parcel. You always hear from us within 24 hours.

How much does a map cost?

MTB areas and routes on a single plate, such as Rijk van Nijmegen and the Geile Strecke, cost €45. The large maps, such as the Green, Brown and Dutch Divide, the Elfstedentocht and the Amstel Gold Race, cost €55. The extra-wide maps, the Utrechtse Heuvelrug and the TransBelgian (47 cm), cost €65. For your own ride you get a price up front. Shipping is €5 per order. Maps that are only shown as a 3D render ship for free.

Why do I get free shipping?

There are two reasons:

  • You order two or more maps in one order. They travel together in one parcel, so shipping is free. Just add another map in the order form.
  • The map has not been printed before. For such a map the site still shows a 3D render instead of a photo, and we ship it for free. See why some maps show a 3D render for the details.
Can I have my own ride printed?

Yes. Upload your GPX file in the order form (you can export it from Strava, Komoot or Garmin Connect, for example). We get in touch within 24 hours. We match your ride to real elevation data and send you a preview before we print. You get that preview within 1 to 3 working days. Once you approve it, your map ships within 3 to 5 working days.

How does the ride card work?

Gravel and road routes come with a separate ride card that slots into the map, showing your date and total ride time. You can see straight away what it looks like with the featured route above.

Why do some maps show a 3D render instead of a photo?

Every map is only printed once you order it. That way you always get a fresh copy made just for you, and no maps sit gathering dust that nobody wants (which also saves a lot of material). For routes that have not been ordered before, we therefore show a 3D render. It is made directly from the print file, so what you see is exactly what you get. As soon as a map is printed for the first time, real photos are added. If you order a map that is only shown as a render, we ship it for free.

Can I hang the map on the wall?

Yes. There are keyholes on the back, so you can hang it on the wall with one or two screws. Of course you can also stand it on a windowsill or shelf.

Can I choose the colours?

Happy to help. Every route has a standard colour scheme, but a different route colour or landscape is often possible.
